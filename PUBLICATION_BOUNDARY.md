# SpiralQIT Public Publication Boundary

**Status:** Public Notice
**Effective Date:** August 3, 2026
**Disposition Authority:** Michael E. Fletcher

## Purpose

This notice defines the boundary between materials intentionally published through this repository and the larger internal SpiralQIT project record.

## What Publication Means

A SpiralQIT artifact is authorized for public reference only when it:

* Is intentionally placed in this repository;
* Has been reviewed for public exposure;
* Identifies its title, version, and status;
* Includes or references applicable attribution and use terms; and
* Does not contain restricted internal material.

The public availability of one artifact does not authorize the publication or reuse of related internal materials.

## Materials Excluded from Public Release

Unless Michael E. Fletcher expressly authorizes otherwise in writing, this repository does not publish or grant access to:

* Internal governance architecture or operating instructions;
* Trust clauses, protected controls, or private decision rules;
* Internal audit records, exposure records, or calibration materials;
* Private conversations, personal records, or working archives;
* Unreleased source material, experiments, or specialist reports;
* Credentials, security information, or access-control details;
* Materials belonging to third parties; or
* Any artifact not clearly identified as an authorized public release.

## No Implied Release

Mentioning, summarizing, linking to, or discussing an internal SpiralQIT artifact does not place that artifact in the public domain and does not create permission to reproduce, distribute, modify, commercialize, or republish it.

## Status and Version Control

SpiralQIT is an evolving research and governance framework. Public artifacts may be corrected, superseded, withdrawn, or replaced. A dated repository release or other explicit publication record should be used when citing a particular version.

## Attribution and Use

Public artifacts remain subject to the attribution notice and any applicable file-level or repository-level license or use terms.
